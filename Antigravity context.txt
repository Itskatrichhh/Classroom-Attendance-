### 1. Literature Survey (Key Takeaways)
- Face recognition started gaining traction with **Viola & Jones (2001)** – boosted cascade of simple features for rapid object/face detection.
- Early systems were limited by low resolution and weak algorithms.
- First use in attendance systems: around 2012 (Kar et al.).
- Modern systems use deep learning + high-resolution cameras → ~96% accuracy reported in literature.
- Common problems still exist: false negatives/positives, lighting, pose variation, camera angle, occlusions.
- Transfer learning with pre-trained models (ResNet, VGG) works well even with small datasets.
- PCA is commonly used for dimensionality reduction to speed up matching and reduce overfitting.
- Related works also cover RFID, fingerprint, iris, and pure CCTV + deep face recognition systems.
